# History of Central Processing Units (CPUs)

- The form, design, and implementation of CPUs have changed over time, but their fundamental operation remains almost unchanged.
- Principal components of a CPU include the
	- ALU (Arithmetic-logic-unit)
	- [[processor registers]] that supply operands to the ALU and store the results of ALU operations
	- control unit that orchestrates the fetching (from memory), decoding, and execution of instructions by directing the coordinated operations of the ALU, registers, and other components.
- Most modern CPUs are implemented on [integrated circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_circuit "Integrated circuit") (IC) [microprocessors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microprocessor "Microprocessor"), with one or more CPUs on a single IC chip.
