**Wizard-of-Oz experiment**: In the field of human–computer interaction, a Wizard of Oz experiment is a research experiment in which subjects interact with a computer system that subjects believe to be autonomous, but that is actually operated or partially operated by an unseen human being. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wizard_of_Oz_experiment))
