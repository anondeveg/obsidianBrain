instead of thinking with branches think of tautologies 

for example an always on gate would be  A OR (NOT A) → always 1 (A is either 1 or its inverse is).

you can convert between the four primary gates with either inverting the input or inverting the output.
	inverting the input of nor would give the result of nand
	inverting the input of or would give the results of and
	inverting the output of and would give nand 
	inverting the output of nand would give and
	inverting the output of or would give nor
	inverting the output of nor would give or
you can go from any of these to the other by inverting either the input of the output or both.
